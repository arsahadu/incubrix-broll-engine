# AI_USE.md

## AI assistance disclosure

-   **ChatGPT** --- development assistance, debugging guidance,
    documentation, and implementation suggestions.
-   **Ollama with Llama 3.2 3B** --- local scene planning.
-   **sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2** --- semantic footage
    relevance ranking.
-   **faster-whisper** --- local transcription of uploaded narration.

## Human work

The project implementation, integration, configuration, testing, and
submission decisions were carried out and reviewed by the candidate.
AI-generated suggestions were used as assistance and should be checked
against the actual code and results.

## Data and services

-   **Pixabay API** --- stock-footage search.
-   **FastAPI, Streamlit, FFmpeg, Docker** --- application and
    media-processing infrastructure; not generative AI models.

## Costs

The project was developed using free-tier cloud hosting on Railway and the Pixabay API's free access tier for stock footage. No paid services were intentionally used. Total cost reflects the development and deployment costs incurred to date, subject to confirmation against actual billing records and usage limits.
